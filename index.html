The Montessori Way

At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, every child is guided through the whole-child approach developed by Dr. Maria Montessori.

Welcome to O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines! We’re a forward-thinking, co-educational school serving students from pre-kindergarten through Grade 12, and we offer a strong alternative to international schools. At OBMC, time-honored teaching meets fresh ideas so that each child is ready for life.

Our mission is to help students grow into self-motivated, capable people who can thrive, lead, and make a difference in a world that never stops changing.
